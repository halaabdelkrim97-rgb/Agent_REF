# Client Delivery

Not legal advice: contracts, payment terms and licenses are the user's decision; point out items to confirm.

## 1. Discovery brief (ask one question at a time, record answers in the context file)
Goal and audience, success measure, must-haves and nice-to-haves, deadline, brand assets, content owner, hosting and domain, browsers and devices, integrations, who approves, what is out of scope.

## 2. Scope and acceptance
Write a one-page scope: deliverables, pages or screens, features, assumptions, exclusions, and acceptance criteria that can be checked ("form sends and shows confirmation; works at 360 px; loads under 3 s on a mid phone"). Mark risks and unknowns.

## 3. Milestones
Split into reviewable steps (structure, design direction, build, content, testing, launch). After each, show something the client can react to. Use `brainstorming` and `writing-plans` for non-trivial parts.

## 4. Changes
Check every new request against the scope. In scope: do it. Out of scope: describe the change, effort and impact, and let the user decide with the client. Log decisions in section 7 of the context file.

## 5. Pre-launch checklist
`testing` (console, responsive, keyboard), `accessibility`, `security-review`, `seo-performance`, content and links reviewed, forms tested, analytics and cookie consent as agreed, backups, 404 and redirects, favicon, licenses and credits for fonts, images and assets.

## 6. Handover pack
- Source files and how to build or deploy (README from `doc-writer`).
- Hosting, domain and account list, with who owns each. Never write passwords into files; hand them over through the client's secure channel.
- Asset and license list (fonts, images, models, plugins) with usage rights.
- How to edit content, known limits, browser support, maintenance advice, and what to do if something breaks.
- A short walkthrough of the finished work and the acceptance checklist, signed off by the client.

## 7. After launch
Agree a support window, record the final status with `doc-writer`, and note lessons for `reflect`.
