How this works

The motor speed follows the setting with a first-order lag (time constant 0.8 s). Blade angle is the time integral of rpm. Airflow and power are rough illustrative estimates (airflow proportional to rpm, power rising with the cube of speed), not measured data. The 3D model is built from simple shapes.
