# Visual brief prompts

Use these prompts inside project context section 10 for new UI, game or 3D visual work. Keep the answers around 10-15 lines and link longer specifications. Reuse existing decisions; omit irrelevant fields. Do not make another summary file.

- Purpose and primary action: what must the visitor understand or do?
- Reference: exact URL, image or local file and the qualities to borrow; never claim to have inspected an unavailable reference.
- Composition: focal point, hierarchy, whitespace and desktop/mobile behavior.
- Design tokens: palette roles, type scale, spacing, radius and motion; point to their existing definition.
- Assets: model/image paths, provenance, license and approved substitutions.
- 3D only: silhouette, scale, camera/framing, material separation, key/fill/environment lighting and background.
- Motion and controls: meaningful transitions, inputs and reduced-motion behavior.
- Constraints: target devices, existing performance budget and loading/fallback behavior. Mark proposed targets as proposed until agreed.
- Representative sample: one screen/component or composition to polish before expansion.
- Acceptance: 3-5 observable criteria tied to this design, rather than “looks premium.”
- Verification: viewports, important states and screenshot locations; actual results or explicitly not checked.

During review, fix the largest visible mismatch first. Default to two visual correction rounds; then record unresolved criteria and the blocker or next action. Required functional and release checks still apply. Do not treat an iteration limit as approval or a passing result.
